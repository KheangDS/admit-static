Completion test: agreed tools are delivered and reviewed, practical trials are recorded and repeat use is checked where evidence exists. Participant non-submission is reported, not treated as success. Baseline diagnosis is separately scoped when no usable baseline exists; routine end-of-programme reporting is included, while an independent impact evaluation is additional.

Export Market Development Cohort

Reference design: ten enterprises, one shared target market, one product family per enterprise and three to six months. Participants are grouped by preparation, buyer-testing and coordination needs. The proposal specifies each group’s outputs and coaching, shared research, cohort contact totals, allocation rules and external costs.

All participants do not automatically receive equal buyer contacts. Allocation follows readiness and fit with documented reasons. The report separates preparation completed, contacts attempted, observed buyer interest, quotations and orders. Buyer activity starts only for eligible approved offers; blocked enterprises receive the agreed preparation work.
